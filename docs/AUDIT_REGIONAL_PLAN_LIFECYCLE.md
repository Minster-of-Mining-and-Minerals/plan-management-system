## 2. Architectural Design Decision for Regional Replication

To satisfy the non-negotiable architectural boundaries (regional isolation, independent planning domains, account domain separation, and zero regression of federal workflows), we adopt **Approach 3: The Hybrid Domain-Isolated Architecture**:

1. **Physical Table Isolation for Regional Entities:**
   - `regional_plan_versions`: Owned by `region_id NOT NULL REFERENCES regions(region_id)`.
   - `regional_plan_nodes`: Owned by `regional_plan_version_id NOT NULL REFERENCES regional_plan_versions(regional_plan_version_id)`.
   - `regional_planning_configurations`: 1:1 with `regions(region_id)`.
   - `regional_plan_rules` & `regional_plan_rule_steps`: Owned by `regions(region_id)`.
   - `regional_internal_assignments`: Associates `regional_plan_nodes` with `regional_structures(regional_structure_id)` and regional users.
   - `regional_plan_node_action_logs`: Complete audit history for regional node changes.

2. **Zero Federal Query Leakage:**
   - Federal plan queries query only `plan_versions` and `plan_nodes`.
   - Regional plan queries query only `regional_plan_versions` and `regional_plan_nodes`.
   - A regional account or URL cannot accidentally or maliciously query federal data or another region's data.

3. **Reuse of Core Calculation & Workflow Engines:**
   - The pure math algorithms in `planExecutionRollupService.js` (leaf evaluation, recursive weighted/unweighted aggregation, policy capping) and workflow definitions in `planAchievementWorkflowUtils.js` are utilized directly, guaranteeing exact mathematical and business rule parity between federal and regional plans.
